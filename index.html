常见问题

  • 游戏记录会丢吗？ 统计数据保存在本机，并通过你自己的 iCloud 在设备间同步。删除 App 后，只要 iCloud 已登录，重新安装后会自动恢复。
  • 卡住了怎么办？ 点「提示」，它会告诉你下一步用什么技巧、为什么。
  • 想重新看新手教学？ 在「设置 → 帮助 → 重新观看新手教学」。
  • 错误次数限制？ 中等及以上难度默认错 3 次结束，可在设置中关闭；简单难度不限。

FAQ

  • Will I lose my records? Statistics are stored on your device and synced between your devices through your own iCloud. If you reinstall the app while signed in to iCloud, they come back automatically.
  • Stuck? Tap Hint — it shows which technique to use next, and why.
  • Replay the tutorial? Settings → Help → Replay Tutorial.
  • Mistake limit? Medium and above end after 3 mistakes by default; you can turn this off in Settings. Easy has no limit.
